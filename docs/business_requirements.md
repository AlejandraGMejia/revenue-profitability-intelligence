# Business Requirements
## Revenue & Profitability Intelligence

### Business Problem
The company needs a centralized analytical solution
to evaluate sales performance, cost efficiency,
and gross profitability.

### Project Objectives
1. Monitor revenue and gross profit trends.
2. Identify profitable products and customers.
3. Evaluate revenue growth and gross margins.
4. Analyze financial performance by segment.
5. Support scenario-based business decisions.

### Data Source
Microsoft AdventureWorks sample database.

### Target Users
- Executive Management
- Finance Department
- Sales Management
- Business Intelligence Analysts

### Core Deliverables
- PostgreSQL analytical database
- Documented dbt transformations
- Financial KPI definitions
- Four Power BI report pages
- Streamlit scenario analysis application
- Automated data quality tests
- Business insights and recommendations

### Success Criteria
- Reproducible data transformations
- Validated financial calculations
- Tested and documented analytical models
- Interactive dashboards
- Traceable business findings

### Known Constraints
- Sample data represents a fictional business.
- Gross profit may rely on standard-cost estimates.
- Net profit is out of scope without operating expenses.
- Source schema and coverage require validation.
